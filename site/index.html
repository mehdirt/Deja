How it works

It works the way memory should — quietly

Three steps, and you only do the first one. No buttons to remember, no folders to keep tidy — you ask questions the way you already do, and Deja does the remembering.

  1. 1

    Ask like you always do

    Keep using ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Grok. Hit Enter — there’s nothing new to learn and nothing to click.

  2. 2

    Deja saves it — privately

    Every prompt lands in a library on this computer. Searchable, reusable, and never sent anywhere.

  3. 3

    The right one comes back

    Start typing something you’ve asked before and your earlier version floats up — one click to reuse it.

Find it when you half-remember

Search forgives plurals, spellings, and everyday synonyms. Fill in blanks like {topic} when you reuse a prompt that changes each time.

Yours to control

Pause saving in one click, turn Deja off for any site, or let it pause itself in a private window. Skip short throwaways so only the keepers stay.

Yours to keep

Download a readable document or a full backup anytime. Wipe everything in one click. No lock-in, ever.

Privacy

Private by design — not by promise 🔒

Deja can’t leak what it never sends. Everything stays on your computer.

  • No network calls, no tracking, no analytics. Nothing you type ever leaves your machine.
  • No accounts, no cloud, no other companies. Your library lives in your browser, on this device.
  • Only what you type is saved — never the AI’s answers.
  • Personal details can be hidden before anything is saved — emails, phone numbers, cards, and keys become placeholders like [email].
  • Pause anytime. One click stops saving everywhere; private windows are paused by default.
