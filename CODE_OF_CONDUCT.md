# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Remember that most people filing an issue here
aren't developers — they're someone whose prompts stopped saving, doing their
best to describe it.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What's expected

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who can't describe a problem in
  technical terms. "This is a duplicate of #12" costs nothing to soften.
- Giving and taking feedback on the work rather than the person. A review
  comment is about a diff.
- Accepting that a "no" on scope isn't a judgement of you. Deja is deliberately
  narrow, and most rejections are about keeping it that way.
- Respecting the privacy rules even when they're inconvenient — never asking
  someone to paste prompt text or personal data into a public issue to help you
  debug.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission
- Deliberately pressuring someone into sharing prompt contents, screenshots
  containing personal data, or their library
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits, and any place someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/mehdirt/Deja/security/advisories/new)
(it's the private channel this repo has) or contacting the maintainer directly
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially and reviewed as
promptly as a small project can manage.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions
that don't follow this code, and will explain why when they do. Repeated or
severe behaviour leads to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
